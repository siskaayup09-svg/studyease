💡 Stress Management

Healthy strategies you can try during a busy school week.

01 · Prioritize

List everything you need to do, then choose the most urgent or important task first.

02 · Study in manageable blocks

Use short focused sessions and include breaks. A realistic plan is easier to maintain than an overloaded schedule.

03 · Protect your sleep and breaks

Rest is part of maintaining concentration and well-being. Avoid treating every free minute as study time.

04 · Ask for clarification

If an assignment is confusing, asking a teacher early can reduce unnecessary worry and last-minute pressure.

05 · Talk to someone you trust

Sharing what you are dealing with can help you feel supported and find practical next steps.
